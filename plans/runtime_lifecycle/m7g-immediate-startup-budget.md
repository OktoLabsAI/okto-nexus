# M7g — Honor the configured start-wait budget for immediate one-shot claims

Date: 2026-10-18 UTC. Source and isolated installed-wheel qualification passed; this is an intermediate M7 checkpoint, not completion of UI-B.

An ordinary queued one-shot call already retained its configured `queue_timeout_seconds` deadline after admission. The immediate reservation used by automated broadcast handoffs instead capped its `admission_deadline` at 60 seconds, even when the effective policy allowed 300 seconds. A real Pi UI pool burst previously recorded cold `runtime.open` intervals of 89.063 and 65.565 seconds under load. Those were ordinary queued openings, so they do not prove an observed handoff failure, but they show that the fixed immediate cap can expire before a healthy cold opening is ready.

Immediate admission now uses the same effective start-wait budget as queued admission. The deadline remains durable per call; expiry still refuses late dispatch, retains an uncertain or starting physical slot until confirmed disposal, and reports an outcome to the caller. A 300-second policy now permits a 90-second simulated healthy cold startup rather than failing it at the old 60-second cutoff. The one-shot settings UI labels this field **Wait to start timeout** and explains that it includes cold startup after admission. Increasing this budget can keep a starting slot occupied longer; the configured limit and the usual containment rules bound that tradeoff.

The focused source campaign initially passed 36 cases and failed three Connector subprocess cases before their assertions because `-I` loaded an older user-site Connector missing `publish_startup_refusal`. Installing the current Connector 0.0.10 wheel into the disposable test venv made those three source cases pass. The final Nexus 0.3.1 wheel SHA-256 is `f5f26b2e6d29f3658c77ef7c6fb3716df31130cb569f18b5adc3cd53a0913aba`; Nexus and Connector import from that isolated venv's `site-packages`. The final installed wheel passed **39/39** immediate-admission/startup-deadline cases, **4/4** handoff-claim transaction cases, and **24/24** one-shot capacity cases in a single 67-case run. The TypeScript/frontend production build passed; the installed wheel contains the updated UI text.

No global Nexus process was started or changed. A direct real-UI handoff that spends over 60 seconds in cold startup, a wider latency distribution, and the remaining embedded/Connector UI-B matrix are still pending.
