# M7h — Real Pi UI queue and overflow checkpoint

Date: 2026-10-18 UTC. This is partial UI-B evidence, not completion of the full gate.

The disposable installed Nexus 0.3.1 fixture at `127.0.0.1:8204` used the installed Connector 0.0.10 daemon, Core 0.0.12 and real Pi 0.87.1. Through Agents > Connections, the operator set `m6d-pi` one-shot capacity to maximum **1 parallel**, **0 warm**, **1 queued**, with the inherited **300-second wait-to-start** budget. Agents showed the remote Pi Online and the pool at 0/1 available. The global Nexus stayed stopped.

From Meta-harness, four private requests to Pi were attempted with distinct `M7H_QUEUE_[A-D]_OK` markers and a 20-second terminal-wait instruction. The first three sends were accepted. The fourth returned the visible `ONE_SHOT_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED: Pool capacity is exhausted.` alert and did not create a sent message. UI results for A, B and C were exact, and each accepted request eventually showed **1/1 ack**. The first scripted browser attempt sent A but its immediate second form fill was cleared by the first send's UI reset; B, C and D were subsequently sent as separate UI actions. This matters for timing: B was submitted at approximately 02:18:07 UTC while A was still active, C at approximately 02:18:26 while B was opening, and D at approximately 02:18:44 while the one queue slot was occupied.

Read-only durable-operation inspection matches serial physical execution. A's `runtime.open` was created at 02:16:57.639 and reached SUBMITTED at 02:17:30.939; its turn SUCCEEDED at 02:18:16.044 and close SUCCEEDED at 02:18:19.862. B's open began at 02:18:20.935, after A closed, reached SUBMITTED at 02:19:09.910, then its turn and close SUCCEEDED at 02:19:48.865 and 02:19:52.543. C's open began at 02:19:52.982, after B closed, reached SUBMITTED at 02:20:47.877, then its turn and close SUCCEEDED at 02:21:28.025 and 02:21:30.603. No admitted call in this sample lacked a terminal result, and no overlapping Pi opening or turn appeared with the configured parallel limit of one.

The Connector daemon stopped through its CLI. The fixture recorded `DRAINED` with empty resources and error codes, then `lifespan_finished` at 02:21:51 UTC. The browser closed and neither port 8202 nor 8204 listened afterward. This sample covers queue admission, one-slot overflow refusal, release and subsequent progress with one real harness. It does not cover a queue deadline, broader load distribution, embedded comparison or other harnesses; those remain in UI-B.
