# M5k: installed Connector recovery and real Pi handoff completion

The disposable Nexus 0.3.1 fixture at `127.0.0.1:8204` retained the M5j broadcast handoff `hof_c5dfac3d451b486fb57375b56de7558b`. A first Connector reconnect failed because Core still owned capacity for its earlier `LEASE_REVALIDATION_REQUIRED` opening, despite a durable no-effect receipt and no native Pi claim or launch. The Connector 0.0.10 M5k correction released that exact slot before the general owned-slot recovery fence. An installed replacement Connector reconciled all 21 retained operations and reached `CONTROL_READY` on its first attempt without restarting Nexus. Four refused opening slots were `RELEASED`.

The agent's one-shot Connections form showed a five-second “Wait to start timeout”; the same policy was present in Nexus storage. Real cold Pi starts exceeded that deadline, so the subsequent handoff attempts were refused before native execution and automatically reopened. The operator changed the timeout to 300 seconds through the UI and selected Finish. Reopening the form and reading the persisted policy both confirmed 300 seconds.

After its bounded cooldown, the original handoff was claimed by `m6d-pi`. Its one-shot call `op_ea4f4740d6444f32ac5ddc6289e3698e` started about 48 seconds after admission and reached `SUCCEEDED` with `M5J_RECOVERY_OK`. The Handoffs UI displayed `COMPLETED` and the same agent response. The Connector remained `CONTROL_READY` with zero consecutive control failures, and the successful opening's capacity slot was `RELEASED`. This establishes the disposable remote Connector/Pi recovery and completion path. It does not constitute the separate-host or multi-harness qualification still required by the larger plan. Global port 8202 was not used.
