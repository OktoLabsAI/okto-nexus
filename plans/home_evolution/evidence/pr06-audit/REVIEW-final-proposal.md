# HOME-R1 — final coverage proposal

Read-only audit; no ledger promotion. Snapshot from final backend28+2, foundation26, UI28 and boundaries5. Final browser/package/performance work may supersede the pinned preliminary evidence.

`VERIFIED_AT_STATED_LAYER` is a scoped automated assertion, not blanket live/end-to-end acceptance. Suite counts overlap scenario requirements and are never converted into73/73.

## Current M3 priorities

1. **Close final pinned browser and installed-package evidence already in progress** (F04, F07, H01, H03, I04). No new provider needed for these. Do not call SSR focus or CSS viewport a true screen-reader/200% browser-zoom proof.
2. **Retain final performance measurements and explicit missed target before default rollout** (H06, H07, H08). Large fixture exceeds500ms in retained service measurement. Do not rename it PASS; final source rerun and rollout limitation/optimization decision belong to Root.
3. **One genuinely authorized disposable local harness campaign through canonical terminal and Home** (C01, C03, C05, C10, D08, I01, I03). Use a qualified harness and finite work. Actual operator must decide the real question; do not synthesize a human decision/native receipt. Record request/result, exact continuation or explicit closed refusal, interruption scope if selected, close/reopen and presentation-off continuity without extra jobs just for screenshots. HOME-033 requires consent and disposable data; R3 grants/waivers do not authorize this automatically.
4. **Use multiagent/remote and external variants only where actually available and authorized** (B08, G04, G05). Remote qualification is conditional in the plan. Herdr optional, not a new universal gate. Do not invent active sessions to simulate campaign completion.
5. **Keep specific uncovered synthetic/browser cases visible** (A08, B08, B09, C06, E08, H02). Server-wide incident placement, two independent sessions, informational broadcasts, unknown response/two-tab UI and screen-reader announcements are not settled by suite count. Test only the relevant contract when closing each gap.
